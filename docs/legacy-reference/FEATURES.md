# Beetle Pro: Features

- Reference-faithful homepage: hero with pendant lamps and mini cards, product grid, about,
  offer zone, atelier, fresh picks carousel, shop the look with hotspots, shop by category,
  reviews marquee, journal, footer with newsletter.
- Shop with category filters and sort; data-driven categories (Shirts, T-Shirts, Polo, Pants,
  Jeans, Belts, Accessories: extensible).
- Product pages: photo, sizes (S-XL / waist 30-38), stock states, quantity.
- Fly-to-cart: photo folds into a paper plane and flies to the bag (per fly-to-cart-main.zip).
- Bag with order summary; delivery ৳80, free over ৳1,500.
- Checkout: delivery details, payment method (COD, bKash, Nagad, Rocket, Upay, card), order
  confirmation with order number. Payment step is a placeholder until a gateway is connected.
- Account sign-in/sign-up (demo, this visit), wishlist, live search.
- Ink bleed theme changer (per ink-bleed-theme-changer-main.zip): navbar toggle, light/dark,
  choice saved; site never auto-darkens.
- SEO: per-page meta/canonical/OG, JSON-LD (BDT), robots.txt, sitemap.xml, crawler fallback.
